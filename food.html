Kongunadu Biryani

கொங்குநாடு பிரியாணி

A mild, pale biryani: vellai means white. It is made with short-grain seeraga samba rice and chicken or mutton, with far fewer spices than most biryanis.

Kongu specialChicken or mutton

Where to find it

  • Valarmathi Mess · Race Course, next to the Cosmopolitan Club↗
  • Erode Amman Mess · Kamarajar Road, near Hope College↗
  • Kovai Virundhu↗

Named in: Coimbatore Junction: best biryani · Coimbatore Junction: Erode Amman Mess · Swiggy: famous food in Coimbatore

Arisi Paruppu Sadam

அரிசி பருப்பு சாதம்

A one-pot meal of rice and toor dal tempered with onion, mustard seeds and garlic. It is usually eaten with pickle, rasam, curd and papad.

Kongu stapleVeg

Where to find it

  • Meenu Restaurant↗
  • Erode Biryani↗

Our tipAlso on the menu at most Kongu-style messes in the city.

Named in: Swiggy: famous food in Coimbatore

Pallipalayam Chicken

பள்ளிபாளையம் சிக்கன்

Unmarinated chicken fried in very little oil with shallots, coconut and chillies. It is named after the town of Pallipalayam, near Erode.

Kongu specialNon-veg

Where to find it

  • Erode Amman Mess · Kamarajar Road, near Hope College↗
  • Junior Kuppanna · Sarojini Street, Ramnagar↗
  • Haribhavanam · Balasundaram Road↗

Named in: Coimbatore Junction: Erode Amman Mess · Outlook Traveller: Flavours of Coimbatore · Swiggy: famous food in Coimbatore

Ghee Roast Dosa

நெய் ரோஸ்ட் தோசை

A paper-thin, crisp dosa finished with ghee and often folded into a cone. It is served with coconut chutney, sambar and coriander chutney.

BreakfastVeg

Where to find it

  • Annapoorna Gowrishankar · D.B. Road, R.S. Puram↗
  • Sree Annapoorna · several branches, incl. Gandhipuram↗

Named in: Outlook Traveller: Flavours of Coimbatore · Swiggy: famous food in Coimbatore

Paniyaram

பணியாரம்

Dumplings of rice and lentil batter, crisp outside and soft inside. They are eaten at breakfast or with tea, usually with chutneys.

Breakfast & teaVeg

Where to find it

  • Shree Sai Virundhu↗
  • Idly Virundhu↗
  • Salem Ravichandran Tiffin House↗

Our tipEvening street-food stretches like Thirupathi Nagar and Eachanari are good bets too.

Named in: Swiggy: famous food in Coimbatore

Kothu Parotta

கொத்து பரோட்டா

Shredded parotta chopped and tossed on a hot tawa with salna, made with egg or meat or as a vegetable version.

Street foodVeg or non-veg

Where to find it

  • Burma Bhai, Hotel Chicken Choice · near Nehru Stadium↗

Our tipMost evening parotta stalls in the city make it to order.

Named in: Outlook Traveller: Flavours of Coimbatore

Malli Pongal

மல்லி பொங்கல்

A soft, comforting breakfast of rice and moong dal.

BreakfastVeg

Where to find it
