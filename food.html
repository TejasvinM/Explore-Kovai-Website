Kongunadu Biryani

கொங்குநாடு பிரியாணி

A mild, pale biryani: vellai means white. It is made with short-grain seeraga samba rice and chicken or mutton, with far fewer spices than most biryanis.

The story
Coimbatore has its own biryani style, and the Angannan style, named after a much-loved local eatery, is the city's benchmark.
In the pot
Seeraga samba rice with chicken or mutton, a ground paste of shallots and plenty of green chillies, and curd, finished on dum.
How it's eaten
Hot, ideally on a banana leaf, with sliced onions and onion raita.

Arisi Paruppu Sadam

அரிசி பருப்பு சாதம்

A one-pot meal of rice and toor dal tempered with onion, mustard seeds and garlic. It is usually eaten with pickle, rasam, curd and papad.

The story
The everyday one-pot meal of the Kongu region (Coimbatore, Erode, Salem), fondly nicknamed “Gounder's biryani”.
In the pot
Parboiled rice and toor dal cooked soft with shallots, green chillies, garlic, cumin and pepper, finished with a mustard and fenugreek tempering.
How it's eaten
Never without a spoon of hot ghee on top. Gooseberry pickle is a favourite partner.

About the dish: Essence of Life: arisi paruppu sadham

Pallipalayam Chicken

பள்ளிபாளையம் சிக்கன்

Unmarinated chicken fried in very little oil with shallots, coconut and chillies. It is named after the town of Pallipalayam, near Erode.

The story
One of the most popular dishes of the Kongu region, and proof that a few ingredients go a long way.
In the pot
Bone-in chicken roasted dry in sesame oil with plenty of shallots, a fistful of dried red chillies, sliced fresh coconut and curry leaves.
How it's eaten
As a side with rasam rice or curd rice.

Ghee Roast Dosa

நெய் ரோஸ்ட் தோசை

A paper-thin, crisp dosa finished with ghee and often folded into a cone. It is served with coconut chutney, sambar and coriander chutney.

The story
A Coimbatore breakfast staple. The city's Annapoorna group, known for its tiffin, grew from a canteen in an R.S. Puram cinema in the 1960s.
In the pot
A fermented batter of rice and urad dal, spread paper-thin on a hot griddle and roasted in ghee until it crackles.

About the dish: Swiggy: famous food in Coimbatore · Wikipedia: Annapoorna Gowrishankar

Paniyaram

பணியாரம்

Dumplings of rice and lentil batter, crisp outside and soft inside. They are eaten at breakfast or with tea, usually with chutneys.

The story
Known across South India by many names: paddu, kuzhi paniyaram, appe.
In the pot
Rice and urad dal batter, like idli or dosa batter, cooked in a pan with small round hollows. Spiced with chillies, or sweet with jaggery.

About the dish: Wikipedia: Paddu

Elaneer Payasam

இளநீர் பாயசம்

A cool, creamy dessert made with tender coconut.

The story
A tender-coconut dessert from Kerala and parts of Tamil Nadu (karikku payasam in Kerala), made for festivals and weddings.
In the pot
Tender coconut flesh and water blended with sugar, stirred into coconut milk or milk with cardamom and cashews. Often no cooking at all.
How it's eaten
Chilled.

Kothu Parotta

கொத்து பரோட்டா

Shredded parotta chopped and tossed on a hot tawa with salna, made with egg or meat or as a vegetable version.

The story
Tamil Nadu's take on Sri Lanka's kottu roti, a street food since the 1960s–70s. “Kothu” means to chop.
In the pot
Parotta torn into pieces and tossed on a hot griddle with onions and egg or meat, then chopped and mixed with two heavy iron blades.
How it's eaten
Hot off the griddle, as street food.

About the dish: Wikipedia: Kottu

Malli Pongal

மல்லி பொங்கல்

A soft, comforting breakfast of rice and moong dal.

The story
Venn (white) pongal is the savoury pongal. It shares its name with the Pongal harvest festival.
In the pot
Rice and moong dal cooked soft with ghee, black pepper, cumin, ginger, cashews and curry leaves.
How it's eaten
For breakfast, with coconut chutney and coffee.

About the dish: Wikipedia: Pongal (dish)

Where to find it

  • Geetha Café · Gopalapuram, near the railway station↗
